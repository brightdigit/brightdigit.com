Hello everyone!

**AtLeast 1.0.0-beta.7 is out.**

Over the past years, I've had recurring issues with [TMJ](https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/tmj/symptoms-causes/syc-20350941). Along everything that came with it, I needed a way to slow down — to practice my breathing and quiet my mind. A few minutes, a few times a day. The problem was that every timer was built for interrupting rather than letting me continue. I needed a timer for practicing **at least** a few minutes.

That's why I built [**AtLeast, and I'd love for you to try it**](https://atleast.app)**.**

## The timer I actually wanted

**Every timer is built to make you stop.** You set five minutes, a buzzer goes off, you're done. But that's exactly backwards for a breathing exercise — or a plank, a stretch, a run, any exercise where the point is to *keep going*, not stop. I don't want to be interrupted the second I hit the minimum time — I want to be told I've *reached* it, and then left alone to keep going if I feel like it.

**AtLeast** is a timer for a floor, not a countdown: *at least X minutes* of breathing, a workout, focus, reading, movement — whatever. You set the floor, and it taps your wrist toward it instead of yelling at you when time's up.

Every day, I close my eyes, put my ear plugs in, and set it for at least 15 minutes and breathe. It taps my wrist about every minute — *keep going* — and when the taps stop, I know I've done my time. That's the whole interaction, and it's deliberately eyes-free. You feel it; you never look at it. Silence *is* the success state.

What actually matters is reaching a minimum, and once I started using it I kept noticing how many things fit that shape — a minimum stretch of focused work, an *at least* twenty-minute walk, holding a plank until the taps stop, a full stretching routine, reading before bed, a practice session you commit to starting even when you don't feel like finishing.

## What's in beta 7

**Stronger haptics.** Testers kept saying the taps were too soft. Beta 7 adds `.strong` and `.max` weights.

**Accessibility is in this build.** VoiceOver labels and announcements, Reduce Motion that actually follows the system, Dynamic Type on the primary flows, and contrast markers.

Join or update at [**atleast.app**](https://atleast.app).
